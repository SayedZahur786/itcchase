## Service and agreement
ITCChase compares a purchase register with an uploaded GSTR-2B table and prepares supplier follow-up drafts. By using it, you agree to these terms and the Privacy policy. The preview supports one business per browser workspace. It does not file returns, connect to GSTN, verify supplier filing or determine statutory eligibility for input tax credit. Amounts labeled at risk indicate tax on invoices that require review, not tax legally lost or recoverable.

## Eligibility and accounts
Use the service only if you are authorized to process the uploaded business records and are legally able to accept these terms. Access is tied to an essential browser session cookie, not an email account. Reports are available only in that session and may become inaccessible if cookies are cleared. There is no multi-client agency management or account recovery in this preview. Keep the device and session secure and avoid shared computers for financial data.

## Acceptable use
Do not upload records without permission, malware, macro-enabled workbooks or formula-bearing spreadsheets. Do not attempt to access another session, bypass upload limits, overwhelm the service or use supplier drafts for unsolicited bulk messaging. Supported files contain one normalized invoice per row, positive amounts and the specified columns. Credit notes, amendments, reverse charge and eligibility classifications require separate professional review. Do not rely on this tool as your only accounting record.
