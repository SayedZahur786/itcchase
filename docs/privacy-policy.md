# Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

## Who operates this service
ITCChase is a project operated by Sayed Zahur. For support and privacy requests, use the contact page. The Contact form stores your email and message privately for operator review; never include invoice files, GSTINs, session cookies or other private business information. Support requests are retained for 30 days and removed on subsequent submissions; operator replies are manual. You can delete stored reports directly from your Overview page without contacting support.

## Information and purpose
When you run a reconciliation, we process supplier names and GSTINs, invoice numbers, dates, taxable values and tax components from the two files you select. This data is used to compare invoices, group differences and generate supplier follow-up drafts. File contents are processed in server memory and raw uploaded files are not saved. Normalized invoice data and report results are stored to display your monthly overview. Synthetic demo data does not represent actual traders or transactions.

## Cookies and analytics
An essential HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie gives this browser access to its reports for up to 30 days. It is a bearer credential, not a verified identity or firm account. Do not share your browser session. Clearing cookies removes your access but does not immediately erase stored reports. The shared analytics component is disabled: no analytics cookies, marketing trackers or third-party analytics scripts are enabled. We record a count of successfully completed live reconciliations without invoice details in a separate event table. Demo runs do not contribute to this count.

## Service providers and transfers
Vercel serves the application and processes upload requests. Neon Postgres stores normalized reports in the project region recorded in DECISIONS.md. Provider systems may process network and operational metadata in other locations. Rate limiting uses a one-way hash of the hosting platform-provided network address and session identifiers. Opening WhatsApp or an email draft transfers the selected draft to your chosen communication provider only when you activate that link. Review recipient and content before sending; ITCChase does not send messages automatically.

## Retention and security
Reports become inaccessible after 30 days and expired records are deleted during the next successful live reconciliation. This means physical deletion can occur later than 30 days when the service is idle; provider backups may persist according to provider policies. You can immediately delete active reports belonging to your session using Delete my reports in Overview. Event counts are retained for up to 90 days, with cleanup on subsequent runs. Rate-limit records are cleaned after two hours on subsequent runs. Uploads are limited to 2 MB and 5,000 rows per file. Transport encryption, parameterized database queries and session ownership checks reduce risk, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights
Upload only records you are authorized to process. You may stop using the service, delete your reports, or request assistance through Contact. No account recovery exists in this early preview: if you clear the cookie, wait for expiry or contact support without sharing financial data. We do not sell business data or use it for advertising. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location; we will consider legitimate requests and explain any limitations before requesting further information.

## Changes
This policy reflects the current preview. Changes to retention, payment processing or analytics will be described here before those features are activated. The effective date above identifies this version. Review it when uploading new records.
